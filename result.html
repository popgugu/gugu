Local Tips & Before You Book

  • Tipping: 15%–20% is standard for sit-down dining and rideshares.
  • Transit: Use San Diego's MTS Trolley for Downtown/Old Town; rideshares for La Jolla and North Park.
  • Before You Book: Double-check museum operating hours and book popular tickets (Birch Aquarium, USS Midway) in advance.
